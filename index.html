Satisfy Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026. Developer: Just a dev Games. Contact: marclopezmarco469@gmail.com

Summary

Satisfy Lab does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, microphone or camera input. Progress and settings are stored on your device. The app shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob, which may collect a device advertising identifier. Children's use is handled with child-directed ad settings described below.

Data stored on your device

Coins, owned and selected items, progress counters, the last daily gift day, ad pacing counters and settings. This data stays on your device (IndexedDB with a localStorage mirror). Settings > Reset progress deletes progress. Clearing app data removes everything.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner and rewarded video ads. AdMob may access the advertising identifier and device information to deliver and measure ads. The app requests non-personalized ads and tags requests for child-directed treatment. You can remove ads with the one-time in-app purchase; ads are only shown in the places described in the game.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Optional anonymous usage statistics

Only in builds that enable them and only when you leave Settings > Anonymous usage stats on. Event names and counts only, with a random per-launch session id. Otherwise nothing is sent to a server.

Third-party tracking

No third-party analytics SDKs are included besides AdMob described above.

Children

Satisfy Lab is designed for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases are protected by a parental gate.
